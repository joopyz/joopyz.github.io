Chess Reader: PDF Study — Support

Chess Reader turns chess book PDFs into a reading experience built for study: pages are reflowed for your screen, algebraic notation becomes tappable, diagrams are recognized, and a built-in engine can evaluate positions. You can also scan physical books with the camera. Everything runs on your device and works fully offline.

Getting help

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: email jazappstudio@gmail.com. Please mention your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

Where are my books stored? Imported PDFs, scanned pages, and your reading positions stay in the app's local library on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

A move in my book isn't tappable. Recognition quality depends on the book's print and scan quality. You can correct recognized text from the page's long-press menu, and adjust a move's link from the study board's move history strip.

Does the app need an account or a connection? No. There are no accounts, and the app works fully offline.
